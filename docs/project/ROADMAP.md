# Roadmap

The Influence Index is an editorial field guide to people, their ideas, and their
career milestones. Keep profiles and field notes sourced and easy to explore on
desktop and mobile. The original one-pagers, templates, and Library are retired;
preserving their routes is not a product requirement.

No next feature or release schedule is selected here.
[Features](../system/FEATURES.md) describes current behavior;
[Operations](../system/OPERATIONS.md) owns content editing and verification.
[Backlog](BACKLOG.md) holds unresolved candidates. Git and PRs retain delivery
history.
